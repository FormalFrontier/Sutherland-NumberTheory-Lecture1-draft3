# 18.785 Number theory I

## Scope

- Subject area: introductory number theory lecture notes.
- Source pages are transcribed to Markdown with LaTeX math notation.

## Transcription conventions

- Preserve the page's reading order and section/item numbering.
- Use Markdown headings for lecture titles and section headers.
- Put mathematical notation in `$...$` inline math or `$$...$$` display math.
- Render standard rings and fields as `$\mathbf{Z}$`, `$\mathbf{Q}$`, `$\mathbf{R}$`, and `$\mathbf{F}_q$` when they appear in math.
- Keep bibliographic references in bracket form, such as `[3, Chapter 1]`.
- Render theorem-like items with a bold lead-in, for example `**Definition 1.2.**`.
- Preserve footer authorship lines as italic text at the end of the page.
