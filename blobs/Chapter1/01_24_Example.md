**Example 1.24.** The ring $\mathbf{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$ is not a UFD (nor a PID) because it is not integrally closed: consider $\phi = (1 + \sqrt{5})/2 \in \operatorname{Frac}\mathbf{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$, which is integral over $\mathbf{Z}$ (and hence over $\mathbf{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$), since $\phi^2 - \phi - 1 = 0$. But $\phi \notin \mathbf{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$, so $\mathbf{Z}[\sqrt{5}]$ is not integrally closed.
