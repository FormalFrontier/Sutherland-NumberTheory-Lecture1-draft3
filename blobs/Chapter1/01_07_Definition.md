**Definition 1.7.** For a prime $p$ the *$p$-adic valuation* $v_p : \mathbf{Q} \to \mathbf{Z}$ is defined by

$$
v_p\left(\pm \prod_q q^{e_q}\right) := e_p,
$$

and we define $v_p(0) := \infty$. The *$p$-adic absolute value* on $\mathbf{Q}$ is defined by

$$
|x|_p := p^{-v_p(x)},
$$

where $|0|_p = p^{-\infty}$ is understood to be $0$.
