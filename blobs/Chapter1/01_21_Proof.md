**Proof.** Let $A'$ be the integral closure of $A$ in $B$ and let $A''$ be the integral closure of $A'$ in $B$. Proposition 1.20 implies that $A''$ is integral over $A$. Every element of $B \supset A''$ that is integral over $A$ lies in $A'$ (by definition), so $A' \subseteq A'' \subseteq A'$. Thus $A'$ is equal to its integral closure (hence integrally closed) in $B$.
